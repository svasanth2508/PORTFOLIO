01 COMPUTER VISION / FULL-STACK
CURRENT FLAGSHIP

PlateScope

Traffic video → unique registration intelligence.

Browser-only OCR was unreliable on moving traffic footage. PlateScope separates detection from recognition, then combines repeated observations across frames before producing a final plate.

PROBLEM

Blur, small plates, duplicate observations and unstable OCR.

APPROACH

YOLO localization + PaddleOCR + normalization + multi-frame voting.

MY WORK

ANPR pipeline, API integration, aggregation logic and web workflow.

STATUS

Working prototype focused on Indian registration formats.
